# Theme

Where this project's seats take their names. It is the project's to edit: change the canon, the pool or the
citation shape before seating the next crew member (`crew-wake new`). Seats already named keep their names.
The crew kit copied this file from its default theme at `/crew:adopt` and never overwrites it.

**Canon:** characters from *Stargate SG-1* and *Stargate Atlantis*.

**Pool** (unclaimed unless the roster says otherwise): O'Neill, Carter, Daniel, Teal'c, Hammond, Fraiser,
Walter, Siler, Bra'tac, Jonas, Mitchell, Vala, Landry; Sheppard, Weir, McKay, Teyla, Ronon, Beckett,
Zelenka, Lorne, Woolsey, Keller, Ford, Chuck, Todd.

**Citation** (the "Name from" line of the seat card and the roster): who the character is, where from, and
why the name fits, e.g. "Sgt. Siler, *Stargate SG-1*: keeps the gate running, wrench in hand".
